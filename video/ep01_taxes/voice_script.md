# EP01: How Taxes Actually Work, Part 1 (performance script)

**Voice:** Sam, late 20s. A lighter, friendly young-guy tone: the big brother explaining something over the kitchen
table. He talks to *you*, his 15–16-year-old little brother. Calm and warm by default, and never preachy.

**Legend**
- `[emotion]`: how the line is played. It applies until the next tag.
- `(beat)` ≈ 0.25 s · `(pause)` ≈ 0.5 s · `(long pause)` ≈ 0.9 s
- `...`: the voice trails off or holds.
- **CAPS**: stress that word (louder and slightly higher).
- `[whispers]`: close, breathy, quiet.
- `SFX:` sound effects · `ON SCREEN:` what the viewer sees.

---

### 1 · HOOK (0:00)
SFX: soft whoosh as Sam walks in · lo-fi music fades up
ON SCREEN: paycheck **$4,166** counts down → **$3,378**, red **−$788**

> [confident, a little playful] Your first real paycheck... (beat) will be almost eight hundred dollars SMALLER than you think.
>
> SFX: low thud on "eight hundred" · fast ticks while the number drops
>
> [amused] School taught you photosynthesis... (beat) [dry, deadpan] but never this.
>
> SFX: pop · pop (✓ Photosynthesis, ✗ Taxes)
>
> [warm, leaning in] So, little bro... (pause) let me show you where that money goes.
>
> SFX: riser into the next section

### 2 · WHAT ARE TAXES?
SFX: whoosh · ON SCREEN: "WHAT ARE TAXES?", earn → buy → government

> [upbeat, clear] Okay. Taxes are money you pay to the government... (beat) out of what you EARN, (beat) and what you BUY.
>
> SFX: pop · pop · pop
>
> [softer, quick aside] Quick note: this is education, not personal financial advice.

### 3 · YOUR FIRST JOB
SFX: whoosh · ticks · ding · ON SCREEN: **$0 → $50,000 / year**, ÷ 12 → **$4,166 / month**

> [excited, building] Say you land a job paying FIFTY thousand dollars a year. (pause)
> [proud] That's about four thousand, one hundred sixty-six dollars a month.
> [whispers, teasing] Now watch what happens.

### 4 · THE PAY STUB
SFX: whoosh · a click on each line · ON SCREEN: −$330 · −$258 · −$60 · −$140 · Tax Meter climbs to **$788**

> [matter-of-fact, punchy] Federal income tax takes about three hundred thirty. (beat)
> Social Security, two fifty-eight. (beat)
> Medicare, sixty. (beat)
> [mock-annoyed] And your state grabs around one forty.

### 5 · TAKE-HOME
ON SCREEN: **$3,378** in green · the red "TAXES" chunk falls off · **−$788 GONE**

> [slower, letting it land] So out of four thousand, one sixty-six... you actually take home about three thousand, three hundred seventy-eight.
> (pause)
> [stunned, quieter] Seven hundred eighty-eight dollars... gone.
>
> SFX: heavy thud on "gone"
>
> [louder, frustrated] Every single month.

### 6 · SALES TAX
SFX: whoosh · pop · ding · ON SCREEN: receipt **$20.00 + $1.60 = $21.60** · Tax Meter **$789.60**

> [lighter, playful] And it's not just your paycheck. Buy a twenty-dollar hoodie with eight percent sales tax... (beat) and you pay twenty-one SIXTY at the register.

### 7 · WHY?!
SFX: whoosh · pop · ON SCREEN: giant **WHY?!**

> (pause) [skeptical, mimicking his little brother] Now you're probably thinking: why would anyone agree to this?

### 8 · WHERE IT GOES
SFX: whoosh · a pop on each icon · ON SCREEN: roads, schools, firefighters, hospitals, parks, clean water

> [warm, sincere] Because that money builds the stuff you use every single day.
> [building energy, each item quicker] The roads you drive on. Your school. Firefighters, hospitals, parks...
> [softer, smiling] even the clean water from your tap.

### 9 · EVERYONE CHIPS IN
SFX: thud on "alone" · coins · ticks · ding · ON SCREEN: **$0 → $4.9 TRILLION** (FY2024)

> [thoughtful] Nobody can afford a fire truck or a highway alone. (beat)
> [brightening] So millions of people chip in a little... (pause)
> [awed, louder] and together, it adds up to nearly FIVE TRILLION dollars a year in federal taxes.

### 10 · GROUP PROJECT
SFX: whoosh · pop ×5 · ding · ON SCREEN: five people stack blocks · ✓

> [light, friendly, a small laugh] Think of it like a group project... where everyone pays their share so the whole country actually works.

### 11 · YOUR CAREER
SFX: riser → thud · ticks speed up · ON SCREEN: YEAR 1 → 40, **$0 → $378,240** · Tax Meter switches to "lifetime"

> [whispers, conspiratorial] Now here's the part that'll shock you.
> (beat) [serious] At this pace, over a forty-year career...
> [slow, emphatic, louder] you'd pay around three hundred seventy-eight THOUSAND dollars in taxes.

### 12 · THE TWIST
MUSIC: lo-fi cuts out → tension pulse · lights dim, camera pushes in on Sam
SFX: riser → heavy thud on "don't pay" · ON SCREEN: **WHAT IF YOU DON'T PAY?** · Tax Meter **$378,240 + ???**

> (long pause) [quiet, serious] Which brings up the real question.
> (long pause) [whispers] What happens if you just... don't pay?

### 13 · THE BILL
SFX: click · pop ×3 · slow ticks · ON SCREEN: tax bill **$1,000 → $1,357** over 12 months (estimate)

> [tense, a little faster] Say you skip a one-thousand-dollar tax bill. (beat)
> Penalties start stacking. (beat)
> Interest kicks in. (beat)
> [worried, rising] And every month... that number keeps climbing.

### 14 · CLIFFHANGER
SFX: stamp on "scariest" (**OVERDUE**) · riser → thud into the end card

> [lower, ominous, slow] And trust me... the penalties aren't even the scariest part.
> (pause) [knowing, half-smile, softer] That's what I'll show you in part two.
>
> ON SCREEN: **PART 2: What really happens if you don't pay**

made with a quiet, breathy, close-mic treatment, because Chatterbox can't truly whisper.
