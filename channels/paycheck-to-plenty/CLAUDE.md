## 5. Hook rules (Section D) — every video, no exceptions

**D3 finance rules**
1. First sentence (under 5 s) is about the viewer's money. No "hi", no channel name, no "in this video".
2. A concrete dollar number by second 15.
3. Promise one specific result by the end.
4. Title, thumbnail and first line make the SAME promise.
5. Open one question early and answer it after minute 5.
6. Every number in the hook is true and sourced. No fake urgency.
7. Hook paragraph ≤35 words, spoken in under 15 s.
8. Talk to "you", calm and friendly, never shame the viewer.

**D4 scorecards — ship only 8/10 or higher**
- Hook (0/1/2 each): Stakes • Specificity • Speed • Curiosity gap • Truth and match.
- Title (2 each): proven formula from Section 3 • searched topic or famous name • number/twist/concrete detail • under ~60 chars • honest, delivered in full.
- Thumbnail (2 each): readable at 10% phone size • one face/character or one object • ≤3 words • matches the title's promise without repeating it • clear money emotion (shock, relief, smug).

**D5 templates:** "If you [habit], you're losing about $[X] a year, and nobody tells you." • "I was paying $[X] a month for [tools]. Today I pay $0. Here's every free swap." • "Before you sign a [car loan/lease/card], check these [N] numbers. Most people check one." • "You're not bad with money. [System] was designed this way. Let me show you." • "By the end of this video, you'll understand [topic] better than most people who [do it]."

**Hook bank (Section 3):** your money at stake • mistake in progress ("Sam is about to sign a car loan that will cost him $9,000 more than the car.") • cost reveal • nobody-taught-you • simple promise.
What works now (D1): fear of falling behind / hidden enemy; concrete numbers in title and first 15 s; AI + money ("I tried", "laziest way", "free version"); copyable proof (exact tool, steps, result); calm, non-judgmental tone — no panic, no crypto hype.

## 6. Script structure (12 min, ~1,900 words at ~160 wpm)
| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Hook | Stake for the viewer's money, or the mascot about to make a costly mistake. Promise the payoff. |
| 0:20–1:00 | Why it matters | One concrete sourced number. What they'll know by the end. |
| 1:00–4:00 | How it actually works | Simplest explanation through the mascot's situation. One analogy. |
| 4:00–7:00 | The trap / hidden part | What most people don't know. Open loop before each turn. |
| 7:00–10:30 | What to do instead | 3–5 rules/steps with examples. AI videos: show each free tool working on screen. |
| 10:30–12:00 | Recap + next video | One-line recap, mascot's better outcome, point to next video in the series. |

**Compliance in every video:** say once, naturally, "This is education, not personal financial advice" (and in the description) •
mascot / AI voice never presented as a licensed advisor, no fake credentials • cite sources for every number (IRS, Federal Reserve,
CFPB, company pricing pages) in the description • AI-tool videos: check free-tier limits on the recording day; disclose affiliate links.

## 7. Visual system (Sections 3 & 5)

**HOUSE STYLE A (paste into every prompt)**
```
- Clean, bright 2D digital illustration, bold confident outlines, flat colours
  with soft cel shading, friendly semi-realistic proportions (head ~10% larger).
- Palette: off-white, mint green, warm yellow, coral red, navy; green = gain,
  red = loss, gold = key number.
- Backgrounds simple and readable: apartment, office, car dealership, bank,
  phone screen, store. No clutter, no text unless specified. 16:9.
```
- **Mascot:** original everyman (e.g. "Sam", late 20s, hoodie, slightly anxious), in most scenes making real money decisions. Locked design, 6 approved expressions.
- **Optional guide:** calm, smart (e.g. older neighbour). Never presented as a real financial advisor.
- **Money visuals:** cash stacks, receipts, bills, cards, growing/shrinking jar, illustrated charts with one clear line.
- **AI-tool videos:** real screen recordings of the free tools (owner's own footage) mixed with mascot scenes.
- **Colour code:** green gain, red loss, gold key number — always. **Motion:** slow zoom/pan, number counters, simple arrow/line chart animation.
- **Character lock:** fill the sheet → turnaround (front, 3/4, profile, 3 expressions), re-roll until approved → reuse exact text + reference image in every scene → regenerate any off-model image.
- **One image tool with character reference, and stay with it.** Optional subtle motion on 5–10% of shots; keep characters still.

**Scene mix:** character in setting 40% • establishing 10% • object close-up 15% • data/chart 20% • screen recording/document 15%.

## 8. Voice, audio, edit (Section 6)
- Voice: friendly, clear, confident American, "smart friend over coffee". ~155–170 wpm. Short beat before numbers/reveals. Emphasis on numbers, "actually", the trap word.
- Human narrator is the strongest option. AI option: ElevenLabs, one voice for this channel, never changed. Start: Stability 40–55%, Similarity ~75%, Style 15–30%. Generate paragraph by paragraph; re-roll flat lines; check commercial licence.
- Pronunciation list: `channel-bible/pronunciation.md` (tickers, names; phonetic spellings in TTS script).
- Music: light upbeat ambient / lo-fi, changes per section. SFX sparse: cash register, swoosh, pop on numbers.
- Mix: clean voice (light NR, gentle compression, de-esser) • −14 LUFS integrated, true peak < −1 dB • music 20–25 dB under voice with ducking • YouTube Audio Library or licensed music only.
- Edit: new image every 3–6 s • ~150–220 images per 12 min • scale 100→110% or slow pans, alternate directions • hard cuts, quick whip on section change • key numbers big and coloured, 3-word labels • bright, clean colour • export 1080p or 1440p, 30 fps.

## 10. Rules & risks (Section 8)
- Inauthentic-content rules (July 2026): no interchangeable template videos (vary structure with the topic), no shock that doesn't pay off, no AI expert persona giving finance advice.
- Stay safe: original research and own-words scripts; consistent narrator; own art direction and mascot; real screen recordings; sources in every description. Value test: would a viewer miss this video if it vanished?
- Education, not advice (on screen + description) • no fake credentials • every number sourced and current, re-check tax figures yearly • disclose affiliates/sponsors • no crypto hype or get-rich-quick.
- Copyright: own AI illustrations or public-domain/openly licensed (National Archives, LoC, Wikimedia with licence). Never Google Images, news photos, others' footage. Music: Audio Library or licence. Never chart songs.
- Never make videos about faceless channels / making money on YouTube (3.1% breakout; creator-economy niche narrowing).
