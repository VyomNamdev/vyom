# Fine Print Money: Visual Starter Pack

These prompts follow the locked rules in `character_bible.json`. Each one works on its own, so you can paste it straight into any image generator that accepts a reference image plus text (Midjourney `--cref/--sref`, GPT-Image, Flux/Ideogram with image prompt, Leonardo Character Reference, etc.).

> ### ⚠️ Before every generation
> 1. **Attach the reference image(s).** For the turnaround, attach one of your original screenshots. After you approve a Sam turnaround, always attach **that sheet plus one approved scene still**.
> 2. Set **reference/style strength high** (≈0.7–0.85, or `--cw 100` / `--sw 200` style values).
> 3. Paste the **Universal Negative Prompt** below into the negative field. If the tool has no negative field, append it as an `AVOID:` line.
> 4. Review every result against the **Consistency Checklist**. Reject anything with fleshy limbs, noses, 3D shading, or UI overlays. Fix drift by **re-generating with the references attached**, not by adding new style words.

## Universal Style Lock (already embedded in every prompt below)

```text
Hand-inked 2D editorial cartoon illustration, premium animated finance-explainer still. Minimalist stick-limb characters: very large flat cream-white (#F7F3EA) rounded oval heads with bold uniform black ink outlines, no nose, no ears, no shading; faces are only two solid black dot eyes, thick short expressive black eyebrows and a single clean black mouth line. Arms and legs are single thick solid black stick lines (same weight as the outline), smoothly curved, no elbows, no knees, no muscles. Rounded white mitten hands with a separate thumb, simple dark charcoal bean-shaped shoes. Head ≈ 1/3 of body height, flat-colored outfit torso ≈ 1/3, stick legs ≈ 1/3. Characters use flat color fills only. Backgrounds are richly detailed, semi-realistic 2D illustrated vintage environments (1920s–1940s America) drawn with thinner sepia-brown ink lines, subtle crosshatching, soft warm motivated lighting; palette of warm tan, cream, parchment, wood brown, muted olive green, soft amber lamp glow; only ONE high-contrast accent color per frame. 16:9 widescreen, crisp outlines, 10–15% safe margin for zoom/pan.
```

## 0. Sam: Character Turnaround / Reference Sheet

**Use:** Generate this once, pick the best result, and make it your **master reference** for every later prompt.
**Attach:** your original screenshot showing Sam (for example, the living room or stock certificate still).

```text

STYLE: Hand-inked 2D editorial cartoon. Bold uniform black ink outlines, flat color fills, no shading on the face, no 3D, no anime.

SAM'S LOCKED DESIGN (identical in every drawing):
- Head: very large smooth rounded oval, slightly wider than tall, flat cream-white #F7F3EA, bold black outline, no nose, no ears visible, no neck.
- Face: two solid black dot eyes set wide apart, thick short black eyebrows, a single clean black mouth line.
- Hat & hair: charcoal-gray newsboy flat cap #4A4440 with a soft puffy crown, a small round button on top and a short curved forward brim; 3–4 pointed medium-brown (#7A4E2D) bangs on the forehead and small brown tufts at the sides under the cap.
- Outfit: muted olive-green V-neck knit sweater vest #6E7A45 over a white shirt with a crisp pointed white collar; medium-brown shorts #6B5136 ending above the knees.
- Limbs: single thick black stick arms from the vest armholes and stick legs from the shorts. No elbows, no knees, no muscles.
- Hands & feet: white rounded mitten hands with a separate thumb, bold outline; dark charcoal (#2B2826) rounded bean-shaped shoes.
- Signature prop: beige-khaki canvas messenger satchel #C9B48C worn cross-body, strap from his right shoulder to the bag on his left hip.
- Proportions: head ≈ 1/3 of total height, vest+shorts ≈ 1/3, stick legs ≈ 1/3. Child, about 12 years old.

LAYOUT (16:9, left to right):
TOP ROW: three full-body views in a neutral standing pose, arms relaxed, labeled "FRONT", "3/4 VIEW", "SIDE PROFILE". The side profile shows the cap brim pointing forward, the head still a smooth oval with no nose bump, and the satchel visible on the hip.
BOTTOM ROW: six head-and-shoulders expression busts, same size, labeled:
1 "HAPPY": eyebrows relaxed and slightly raised, wide open grin with a white upper-teeth band.
2 "SMUG": eyebrows angled sharply down toward the center, one-sided smirk curling up on one side.
3 "SHOCKED": eyebrows raised high and arched, eyes as small white circles with tiny black pupils, small round "o" mouth.
4 "SKEPTICAL": one eyebrow raised, the other lowered flat, short slanted mouth pushed to one side.
5 "WORRIED": eyebrows tilted up toward the center, thin wavy mouth, one small sweat drop beside the head.
6 "SERIOUS": eyebrows flat and slightly lowered, short straight mouth, eyes looking straight ahead.

Clean, evenly spaced, crisp lines, readable at small size. The only text is the labels listed above.
```

**Turnaround checklist**
- [ ] Every view has the same cap, bangs, olive vest, white collar, brown shorts and satchel.
- [ ] No nose, including in the side profile; the head stays a clean oval.
- [ ] Limbs are thin black sticks in every view.
- [ ] The six expressions differ only in eyebrows, eyes and mouth. Head shape and hat stay identical.

## Consistency Checklist (use for every image)

**Characters**
- [ ] Heads are flat cream-white rounded ovals with a bold black outline: no nose, no ears, no shading, no blush.
- [ ] Faces have only dot eyes (or white circle eyes for shock), thick eyebrows and a single-line mouth.
- [ ] Arms and legs are **single thick black stick lines**, with no flesh, muscles, elbows or knees.
- [ ] Hands are rounded mittens (white; **black for Mr. Vance only**); feet are dark bean shoes.
- [ ] Sam has his charcoal newsboy cap with the button, brown bangs, olive V-neck vest, white collar, brown shorts and beige cross-body satchel.
- [ ] Lena has braided pigtails with bows, a tan patterned dress and a Peter Pan collar.
- [ ] Mr. Hale has his gray handlebar mustache, bald top, brown waistcoat and cream apron.
- [ ] Mr. Vance has black side-parted hair, a charcoal three-piece suit, a red tie and black hands.
- [ ] The expression matches the one requested.

**Scene**
- [ ] The background is richly detailed, semi-realistic 2D illustration with thinner sepia lines than the characters.
- [ ] The palette is warm and earthy, with only one high-contrast accent.
- [ ] Every requested text is spelled exactly and readable, and nothing else is readable.
- [ ] There are no extra foreground characters.
- [ ] The frame is 16:9 with safe margins.
- [ ] There is no YouTube UI, player bar, corner thumbnail inset, watermark, logo or caption.
